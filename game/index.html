Lantern Court

Beat the Duskkeeper. Each side keeps 20 lanterns of life. On your turn you draw up to five cards and gain 3 wicks of energy. Click a card to play it if you can afford its cost.

Resonance: the second card of a suit you play in one turn, and every one after it, gains +2 to its damage, healing and shield. Commit to one suit and it pays.

Read the Duskkeeper: it announces the suit it gathers before it plays, so a Flame turn means damage is coming and a Grove turn means shields. Answer in kind.

Keys: 1–5 play a card, Space ends your turn, M mutes, R restarts after the duel ends.
